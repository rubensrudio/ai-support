# Account and security

Keep your Assinaflow account safe with a strong password and two-step verification.

## Password rules

Your password must have at least 12 characters. We recommend a passphrase that you do not use on any other website.

## Two-step verification

Turn on two-step verification under **Account > Security**. After entering your password, you type a 6-digit code from an authenticator app. When you turn it on, you receive 10 backup codes. Each backup code works only once.

## Failed sign-in attempts

After 5 failed sign-in attempts in a row, your account is locked for 15 minutes. After that, you can try again or reset your password.

## Resetting your password

Select **Forgot password** on the sign-in page. We email you a reset link that is valid for 60 minutes and works only once.

## Sessions

You stay signed in on a device for up to 30 days without activity. You can sign out of every device at once under **Account > Security**.

## How Assinaflow contacts you

Assinaflow never asks for your password, your verification codes or your full card number by email, chat or phone. If someone asks for them, do not share them and report the message to support.
