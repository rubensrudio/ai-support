# Orders and delivery

Every renewal creates an order for that month's box. This guide explains order statuses, shipping times and tracking.

## Order statuses

Each order has one of these statuses:

- **Pending**: the order was created and the payment has not been confirmed yet.
- **Paid**: the payment was confirmed and the box is being prepared.
- **Shipped**: the box was handed to the carrier.
- **Delivered**: the carrier confirmed the delivery.
- **Cancelled**: the order was cancelled and will not be shipped.

## Cancelling an order

An order can be cancelled only while it is pending or paid. Once an order is shipped, it can no longer be cancelled. Cancelling an order affects only that box: your next renewal happens as usual. An order cancelled before it ships is refunded in full, as described in the Refund policy.

## When your box ships

Boxes are prepared and shipped within 2 business days after the renewal charge is confirmed.

## Delivery times

- Standard delivery: 3 to 5 business days after shipping.
- Express delivery: 1 to 2 business days after shipping, for USD 9.90 per box. Express delivery is included in Premium.

## Tracking your box

We email you a tracking code within 24 hours after your box ships. You can also follow every order under **Account > Orders**.

## Changing the delivery address

You can change your delivery address under **Account > Addresses**. The new address applies to every order that has not shipped yet. A shipped order cannot be redirected.

## Failed delivery attempts

The carrier makes 2 delivery attempts. After that, the box waits at a pickup point for 5 days and then returns to us. We reship a returned box once at no cost.
