# Damaged or missing items

Sometimes a box arrives with a damaged item, or an item is missing. Here is how to report it.

## Reporting a damaged item

Report a damaged item within 48 hours after delivery under **Account > Orders > Report a problem**. Include at least 1 photo of the damaged item and of the box.

## Reporting a missing item

If an item listed on the packing slip is not in the box, report it within 48 hours after delivery under **Account > Orders > Report a problem**.

## Replacement

After we approve your report, a replacement item ships within 5 business days at no cost. You do not need to return the damaged item.

## Box marked as delivered but not received

If tracking shows your box as delivered but you did not receive it, contact support within 7 days of the delivery date. We investigate with the carrier and reply within 3 business days.

## Preferring a refund

If you prefer a refund instead of a replacement, the Refund policy applies: a delivered order is refundable only if it was delivered no more than 7 days ago.
