# Getting started with Assinaflow

Assinaflow is a subscription service that delivers a curated box of specialty coffee, tea and snacks to your door every month. This guide covers the first steps after you sign up.

## Create your account

1. Go to the Assinaflow website and select **Sign up**.
2. Enter your name, email address and a password with at least 12 characters.
3. Confirm your email address with the link we send you. The link is valid for 24 hours.

## Choose your subscription tier

Assinaflow offers three tiers: Essential, Plus and Premium. You can compare items per box, shipping and prices in the Plans and pricing guide. All prices are in USD.

## Your first box

Your first charge happens on the day you subscribe. Your first box is prepared and shipped within 2 business days after that charge, and you receive a tracking code by email within 24 hours after it ships.

## Your renewal date

Your subscription renews every month on the same day of the month you subscribed. If that day does not exist in a given month (for example, the 31st), the renewal happens on the last day of that month. Each renewal charge is made at 06:00 UTC.

## Getting help

Atlas, the Assinaflow virtual assistant, answers questions 24 hours a day in Portuguese and English. Human agents are available Monday to Friday, from 08:00 to 20:00 UTC.
