# Billing and payments

This guide explains when Assinaflow charges you, which payment methods we accept and what happens when a payment fails.

## When you are charged

Your subscription is charged on your renewal date, at 06:00 UTC. The renewal date is the same day of the month on which you subscribed. Every charge is in USD.

## Accepted payment methods

- Credit card.
- Debit card.
- Assinaflow gift balance.

You can update your payment method at any time under **Account > Billing**. The new method is used from the next charge.

## Invoices

We email an invoice within 24 hours after each charge. All invoices are also available under **Account > Billing** for 5 years.

## Failed payments

If a charge fails, we retry it 1, 3 and 5 days after the renewal date. While a payment is pending, your box is not shipped.

If all 3 retries fail, your subscription is suspended. To reactivate it, update your payment method: we charge the amount due immediately and ship your box within 2 business days.

## Duplicate charges

If you see the same charge twice for the same billing period, contact support. After we confirm the duplicate, the extra charge is refunded in full within 7 business days.
