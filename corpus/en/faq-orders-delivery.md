# Orders and delivery FAQ

Frequently asked questions about boxes, shipping and refunds of orders.

## When does my box ship?

Within 2 business days after the renewal charge is confirmed.

## How long does delivery take?

Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days. Express delivery takes 1 to 2 business days and costs USD 9.90 per box, or is included in Premium.

## How do I track my box?

Use the tracking code we email within 24 hours after shipping, or open **Account > Orders**.

## Can I cancel an order?

Yes, but only while the order is pending or paid. A shipped order can no longer be cancelled.

## Can I get a refund for a box?

Only if the order was cancelled before it ships or was delivered no more than 7 days ago. The refund is the full amount of the order, in USD.

## How long does a refund take?

Card refunds are credited within 7 business days after approval.

## My box arrived with a damaged item. What now?

Report it within 48 hours after delivery under **Account > Orders > Report a problem**, with at least 1 photo. A replacement ships within 5 business days at no cost.

## Can I change the delivery address of an order?

Yes, while the order has not shipped. A shipped order cannot be redirected.
