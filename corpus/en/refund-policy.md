# Refund policy

This policy defines when an Assinaflow order can be refunded and how the refund is paid. The same rule is applied by our support team and by Atlas, the virtual assistant.

## Eligibility rule

An order is eligible for a refund only in one of these two cases:

- The order was cancelled before it ships, that is, while its status was pending or paid.
- The order was delivered no more than 7 days ago. The 7 days are counted from the delivery date, inclusive: a box delivered on the 3rd can be refunded through the 10th.

No other order is eligible. In particular, an order that has shipped but has not been delivered yet, and an order delivered more than 7 days ago, are not eligible.

## Refund amount

An eligible refund is always the full amount of the order, including shipping, in USD. There are no partial refunds of an order.

Example: a Plus box of USD 34.90 delivered on the 3rd and reported on the 9th is refunded USD 34.90.

## How the refund is paid

- The refund goes back to the original payment method.
- Card refunds are credited within 7 business days after approval.
- Orders paid with gift balance are refunded to the gift balance.

## What is not covered

- Subscription charges for the current billing period are not refunded pro rata.
- Shipping upgrades are refunded only as part of an eligible order.
- Account credit, loyalty points and gift codes are not converted into cash.

## How to request a refund

Request a refund under **Account > Orders**, by selecting the order and **Request refund**, or ask Atlas in the chat. You receive a confirmation email when the refund is approved.
