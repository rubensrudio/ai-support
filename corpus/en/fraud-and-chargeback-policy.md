# Fraud and chargeback policy

Internal policy for the Assinaflow support and billing teams.

## Chargebacks

When a customer opens a chargeback with their card issuer, the subscription is suspended until the dispute is resolved. No box is shipped while the subscription is suspended.

## Signals reviewed by the billing team

- 3 or more different cards added to the same account within 24 hours.
- A delivery address change followed by a tier upgrade on the same day.
- More than 2 refund requests in 30 days.

## Review

The billing team reviews flagged accounts within 2 business days. During the review, orders that are pending or paid stay on hold.

## Account changes

Changes to the email address or to the password require a confirmation link sent to the current email address. Support agents never change an email address on request in the chat.
