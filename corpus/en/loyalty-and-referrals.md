# Loyalty points and referrals

Assinaflow rewards you for every renewal and for every friend you bring.

## Earning points

You earn 1 loyalty point for every USD 1 charged on your subscription. Points appear in **Account > Rewards** after the charge is confirmed.

## Using points

Every 100 points become a USD 5 discount on your next renewal. You can apply the discount under **Account > Rewards** at least 24 hours before your renewal date.

## Points expiration

Points expire 12 months after they are earned. Points do not expire while your subscription is paused.

## Referring a friend

Share your referral link from **Account > Rewards**. Your friend gets USD 10 off the first box, and you get a USD 10 credit after your friend's first box is delivered. You can earn credit for up to 10 referrals per calendar year.
