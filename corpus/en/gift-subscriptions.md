# Gift subscriptions

A gift subscription sends Assinaflow boxes to someone else for a fixed number of months.

## Gift lengths and prices

Gift subscriptions last 3, 6 or 12 months and are paid upfront, in USD.

- 3 months: the monthly price of the tier multiplied by 3, with no discount.
- 6 months: 5% off the monthly price.
- 12 months: 15% off the monthly price.

## How the gift works

After the purchase, you receive a gift code by email. The recipient redeems it under **Account > Gifts**. A gift code must be redeemed within 12 months of the purchase.

## Shipping

The first gift box ships within 2 business days after the code is redeemed. The recipient receives a tracking code by email for every box.

## End of the gift

A gift subscription does not renew automatically. The recipient receives a reminder 7 days before the last box ships and can then choose to subscribe with their own payment method.

## Gift codes and balance

Gift codes cannot be exchanged for cash. A gift balance can be used to pay for any Assinaflow subscription.
