# Switching between plans

You can move to a higher or a lower tier from **Account > Subscription > Change tier**. You can switch once per billing period.

## Upgrading

An upgrade takes effect immediately. You pay a prorated charge for the days left in the current billing period, based on the price difference between the two tiers.

Example: you are on Essential (USD 19.90) and move to Plus (USD 34.90) with 15 of 30 days left in the billing period. The difference is USD 15.00, so the prorated charge is USD 7.50. Your next box already has 7 items.

## Downgrading

A downgrade takes effect on your next renewal date. There is no charge or credit for the current billing period, and boxes already paid for keep their current number of items.

## Annual billing

With annual billing, upgrades are prorated by the months left in the 12-month term. Downgrades take effect at the next annual renewal.
