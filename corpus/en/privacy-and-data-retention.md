# Privacy and data retention policy

This policy describes the personal data Assinaflow collects, why we collect it and how long we keep it.

## Data we collect

- Account data: name, email address and delivery addresses.
- Payment data: the last 4 digits of your card and its expiry date. Full card numbers are stored only by our payment processor.
- Order history, support conversations and your box preferences.

## How we use your data

We use your data to deliver your boxes, charge your subscription, answer support requests and prevent fraud. We do not sell your personal data.

## Retention periods

- Account data is deleted within 30 days after you ask us to delete your account.
- Invoices are kept for 5 years to meet tax obligations.
- Support chat transcripts are kept for 90 days.

## Your rights

You can request a copy of your data under **Account > Privacy**. We send the export within 15 days of the request. You can also correct your data or ask us to delete it.

## Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Only employees who need the data for their work can access it.

## Contact

Send privacy questions to the privacy team through **Account > Privacy > Contact us**. We reply within 15 days.
