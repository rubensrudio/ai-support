# Account FAQ

Frequently asked questions about signing in, security and your personal data.

## How do I reset my password?

Select **Forgot password** on the sign-in page. The reset link we email you is valid for 60 minutes.

## Why is my account locked?

After 5 failed sign-in attempts in a row, the account is locked for 15 minutes.

## How do I turn on two-step verification?

Go to **Account > Security** and follow the steps with an authenticator app. Keep your 10 backup codes in a safe place.

## How do I change my email address?

Under **Account > Profile**. We send a confirmation link to your current email address before the change takes effect.

## How do I delete my account?

Under **Account > Privacy**. Your account data is deleted within 30 days after the request. Invoices are kept for 5 years.

## In which languages can I get support?

Atlas answers in Portuguese and English, 24 hours a day.

## How do I talk to a person?

Ask Atlas to transfer you to an agent. Human agents work Monday to Friday, from 08:00 to 20:00 UTC.
