# Pausing your subscription

If you are traveling or simply need a break, you can pause your subscription instead of stopping it.

## How long you can pause

You can pause for 1, 2 or 3 months. You can use one pause every 12 months.

## How to pause

1. Go to **Account > Subscription**.
2. Select **Pause** and choose the number of months.
3. Confirm the pause.

The pause must be requested at least 24 hours before your next renewal date. Otherwise, it starts after the next renewal.

## While your subscription is paused

- You are not charged and no box is shipped.
- Your loyalty points do not expire.
- Your renewal date stays on the same day of the month.

## Resuming

Your subscription resumes automatically at the end of the pause, on your usual renewal date. You can also resume it early from **Account > Subscription**: the charge happens on the day you resume and that day becomes your new renewal date.
