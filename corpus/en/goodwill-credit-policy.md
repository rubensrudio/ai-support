# Goodwill credit policy

Internal policy for the Assinaflow support team. Goodwill credit compensates customers for service failures that are not covered by the Refund policy.

## When to offer credit

An agent may offer goodwill credit when:

- A box arrives more than 3 business days after the estimated delivery date.
- A replacement item ships later than promised.
- A support case breaks its response target.

## Credit limits

- An agent may grant up to USD 15 of account credit per customer, once every 6 months.
- Credit above USD 15 requires approval from a support supervisor.
- Goodwill credit never replaces an eligible refund.

## How credit is applied

Credit is applied as a discount on the customer's next renewal. Credit is not paid in cash and cannot be transferred to another account.

## Recording the credit

Every credit must be recorded in the support case with the reason and the amount in USD.
