# Plans and pricing

Assinaflow has three subscription tiers. Every price is in USD and is charged once per billing period.

## Monthly prices

| Tier | Price per month | Items per box | Shipping per box |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Essential | USD 19.90 | 4 | USD 6.90 |
| Plus | USD 34.90 | 7 | Free |
| Premium | USD 54.90 | 10 | Free, express included |

## What each tier includes

- **Essential**: 4 curated items per box and standard shipping for USD 6.90 per box.
- **Plus**: 7 curated items per box and free standard shipping.
- **Premium**: 10 curated items per box, free express shipping and priority human support.

## Annual billing

You can pay for 12 months upfront and get a 15% discount on the monthly price:

- Essential: USD 202.98 per year.
- Plus: USD 355.98 per year.
- Premium: USD 559.98 per year.

Annual billing renews automatically every 12 months on the date you chose annual billing.

## Taxes

Sales taxes depend on your delivery address. They are calculated at checkout and shown separately on every invoice.

## Price changes

If a price changes, we notify you by email at least 30 days before the new price applies to your next renewal.
