# Ending your subscription

You can end your Assinaflow subscription at any time, without fees and without talking to an agent. This guide explains how to end subscription renewals and what happens next.

## How to end subscription renewals

1. Sign in to your Assinaflow account.
2. Go to **Account > Subscription**.
3. Select **End subscription** and confirm.

You receive a confirmation email right away, with a confirmation code for your records.

## Deadline before your renewal date

To avoid the next charge, end your subscription at least 24 hours before your next renewal date. Renewals are charged at 06:00 UTC on the renewal date.

If you end your subscription less than 24 hours before the renewal date, that renewal is still charged and its box is still shipped. Your subscription stops after that billing period.

## What happens after you end your subscription

- You keep your benefits until the last day of the current billing period.
- Boxes that are already paid for are shipped as usual.
- Charges for the current billing period are not refunded pro rata. Refunds for individual boxes follow the Refund policy.
- With annual billing, the subscription stops at the end of the 12-month term and the remaining months are not refunded.

## Loyalty points

Your loyalty points are kept for 30 days after your subscription stops. If you subscribe again within those 30 days, your points are restored.

## Coming back

You can start a new subscription at any time from **Account > Subscription**. Your new renewal date is the day you subscribe again.
