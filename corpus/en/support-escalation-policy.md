# Support escalation policy

Internal policy for the Assinaflow support team. It defines when a conversation moves from Atlas, the virtual assistant, to a human agent, and the response targets for each level.

## Support levels

- **Level 1**: Atlas and the general support agents. They answer questions about tiers, billing, orders and accounts.
- **Level 2**: the billing team. It handles duplicate charges, failed refunds and payment disputes.
- **Level 3**: the operations team. It handles carrier incidents, lost boxes and security incidents.

## When Atlas hands over to a human

Atlas transfers the conversation to a Level 1 agent when:

- The customer asks to talk to a person.
- The customer reports a security problem with the account.
- The question is not covered by the knowledge base.
- The customer remains unhappy after 2 answers on the same topic.

## Response targets

- Premium customers: first human reply within 2 business hours.
- Essential and Plus customers: first human reply within 1 business day.
- Level 2 and Level 3 cases: update to the customer at least every 2 business days until the case is closed.

## Business hours

Human agents work Monday to Friday, from 08:00 to 20:00 UTC. Conversations received outside business hours are answered from the next business day.

## Closing a case

A case is closed when the customer confirms the solution or after 5 business days without a reply from the customer.
