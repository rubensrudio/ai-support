# Billing FAQ

Frequently asked questions about charges, payment methods and invoices.

## When am I charged?

On your renewal date, at 06:00 UTC. The renewal date is the same day of the month on which you subscribed.

## In which currency are prices charged?

Every Assinaflow price and charge is in USD, in both the Portuguese and the English versions of the service.

## Which payment methods do you accept?

Credit card, debit card and Assinaflow gift balance.

## What happens if my payment fails?

We retry the charge 1, 3 and 5 days after the renewal date. If all 3 retries fail, the subscription is suspended until you update your payment method.

## Where can I find my invoices?

Under **Account > Billing**. We also email each invoice within 24 hours after the charge, and invoices stay available for 5 years.

## I was charged twice. What should I do?

Contact support. After we confirm the duplicate charge, it is refunded in full within 7 business days.

## Are taxes included in the price?

No. Sales taxes depend on your delivery address and are shown separately on each invoice.

## Can I get money back for the current month?

Subscription charges for the current billing period are not refunded pro rata. Individual boxes can be refunded under the Refund policy.
